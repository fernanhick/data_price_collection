## Overview

Not all sneakers are created equal. **Collectible sneakers** command premiums based on:
- Scarcity (limited releases)
- Heritage (vintage/retro status)
- Designer prestige (luxury brands)
- Collaborations (hype factor)
- Condition (pristine = higher value)

## Value Drivers

### 1. Scarcity
- **Ultra Limited** (100-500 pairs): 3-10x retail
- **Limited** (500-2000 pairs): 2-5x retail
- **Standard** (2000+ pairs): 1-2x retail

### 2. Condition
- **Deadstock (DS)**: +50-200%
- **Worn** (7/10-9/10): 0% (baseline)
- **Heavily Used** (5/10): -40-50%
- **Poor** (<5/10): -60-80%

### 3. Demand Drivers
- **Hype** (new collab launch): 2-5x retail
- **Artist/Celebrity** (worn/signed): 3-10x retail
- **Anniversary** (retro release): 1.5-3x retail
- **Discontinued** (no longer made): 2-8x retail

### 4. Regional Premium
- Japan exclusive: +20-50%
- Europe exclusive: +15-30%
- US limited: +10-20%

- Expected ROI: 1.5-3x retail

**Tier 3: Enjoyment Grade** (Wearable)
- Current season drops
