# RFC-000: Product Context (Consumer App)
Status: Accepted

## Product Type
Consumer-facing mobile application.

## Core User Expectation
Users want a **simple, credible, and current estimate** of sneaker market value.

## Non-Goals
- Not an official marketplace
- Not a trading or escrow platform
- Not an authoritative price oracle

## Constraints
- No StockX API access
- No paid third-party data
- High legal and platform scrutiny

## Guiding Principle
Transparency, defensibility, and resilience outweigh raw price precision.
